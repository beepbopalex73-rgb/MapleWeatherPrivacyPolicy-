1 Introduction

Maple Weather is a weather application. This Privacy Policy explains how Maple Weather handles information when you use the app.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and explaining how information is used to provide weather forecasts, weather maps, alerts, and Premium subscription features.

2 Information and Location

Maple Weather may use your device's location, with your permission, to provide local weather conditions and forecasts. You can manage or disable location access through your device's settings. You may also be able to search for a city manually.

The app's actual handling of location information depends on its configuration. We will disclose whether location information is sent to a service provider, retained, or processed only on your device.

3 Weather Information and Third-Party Services

Maple Weather may request weather forecasts, precipitation information, road conditions, maps, and weather alerts from external service providers. These providers may receive technical information needed to respond to requests, such as an IP address or requested location, according to their own privacy policies.

The specific providers used may change as the app develops.

4 Premium Subscriptions and Payments

Maple Weather offers optional Premium subscriptions, including monthly and yearly plans. Purchases are processed through Apple's in-app purchase system.

Apple handles payment information and subscription billing under its own terms and privacy policy. Maple Weather uses the purchase information made available by Apple to determine whether Premium features should be available.

Maple Weather does not receive your full payment card details through Apple's in-app purchase system.

5 Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to deliver weather features, operate the app, maintain security, or support subscriptions. We will update this policy if the app's data-sharing practices change.

6 Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the service involved.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7 Children's Privacy

Maple Weather is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, contact us using the details below.

8 Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can manage the app's location permissions through your device settings. You may contact us to ask questions about personal information handled by Maple Weather or to request its deletion where applicable.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on our website with a revised effective date.

10 Contact

For privacy questions or requests regarding Maple Weather, contact us through the contact information published on the Maple Weather website.
